# Sekelas Cafe — Cafe Operations & POS System (SPEC)

Single-tenant web app running the full counter-to-kitchen workflow of Sekelas Cafe, the student-operated teaching-factory cafe of SMKN 1 Boyolangu (Jl. Ki Mangunsarkoro VI/3, Beji, Boyolangu, Tulungagung, East Java): no-login customer self-ordering, cashier terminal, gated kitchen queue, recipe-level stock, Excel data management with Z-reporting, and fully audited RBAC. Baseline stack: Next.js 16 App Router + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4 + shadcn/ui (New York), Prisma + SQLite (`/db`), socket.io mini-service for realtime, SheetJS for Excel. Spec language: English; shipped UI + receipt language: Bahasa Indonesia; locale `id-ID`; timezone `Asia/Jakarta`.

## 1. Important Information

### 1.1 Vision & Jobs-to-be-Done
- Replace paper tickets, shouted orders, manual cash counting, and verbal stock knowledge with one auditable system sized for a school cafe — not an enterprise SaaS.
- **Admin (teacher/supervisor):** "Run and audit the cafe without standing behind the counter; close every business day with provable numbers; onboard a new student cohort in minutes."
- **Cashier (student, rotating):** "Take an order, take cash/QRIS, hand over the right two receipts, verify a QRIS claim without guessing, and close my shift without mistakes."
- **Barista (student, rotating):** "See exactly what to make next, never mark an order complete by accident, record what stock I consumed or wasted."
- **Customer (student/teacher/guest):** "Order from my phone in under 60 seconds without an account, know when my drink is ready."

### 1.2 Scale & Archetype
- One outlet, one tenant, on-campus. Hardware reality: 1 cashier tablet/terminal, 1 kitchen screen (KDS), 1 optional ready-board display, customers on phones, 80mm thermal printers (verified domain recommendation for both kitchen tickets and counter receipts; ~42–48 chars/line vs 32 on 58mm).
- Staff: 3–6 students per shift + 1 supervising Admin; high semester-bound turnover is the defining constraint — drives PIN logins, guided wizards, practice mode, and per-action attribution.
- Volume assumption: ≤ ~300 orders/day with class-break spikes; ≤ 10 concurrent staff sessions.
- Deliberately OUT (scale discipline): multi-outlet, multi-tenant, SaaS billing, payroll, accounting ledger, fiscalization, loyalty/CRM, reservations/floor plans, third-party delivery marketplaces, payment gateway, SMTP, government integrations, offline-first sync engine.

### 1.3 Hard Constraints (owner input, non-negotiable)
- Operating hours default **07:00–14:00, Monday–Friday**; weekends closed; stored as editable per-day settings; a live open/closed state gates ordering and drives Z-report business dates.
- Four roles: **Admin, Cashier, Barista** (staff, authenticated) + **Customer** (never authenticated).
- Two receipt types: **kitchen ticket (KOT)** and **customer receipt**; both reprintable.
- **Kitchen gate:** the "Complete" control stays disabled until every item unit of the order is individually checked off.
- Product **variants**: size, sugar level, ice/hot, toppings, etc. — modeled as per-product variant groups with price deltas.
- Payments: **QRIS (static merchant QR, no gateway → manual confirmation protocol)** and **cash**; split cash+QRIS allowed; no card, no e-wallet API.
- Service types: **Dine-in** (optional table label), **Take-away**, **Delivery inside SMKN 1 Boyolangu grounds only** — flat **Rp 1.000 per ORDER** (never per item), editable in settings.
- Identity: palette **espresso brown + caramel gold**; contact **0851 5182 3036** (WhatsApp `wa.me/6285151823036`).
- Excel management page must generate Z-reports/daily reports and support **import, export, and templates for both inserting new records and updating existing ones (upsert)**.
- Everything mutable is CRUD-managed; pages are intentionally shared across roles (see §1.8) even where permission levels differ.

### 1.4 Ecosystem & Saturation Findings (all verified this session)
- **Indonesian commercial POS:** Moka (mokapos.com — variants/modifiers/photos, ingredient inventory tier-gated, paid employee slots, QRIS+e-wallet), Majoo (majoo.id — KDS sold as separate product, 80mm printer hardware), Qasir (qasir.id — warung-scale pricing, offline recording with auto-sync, stock history with per-change attribution, per-shift cash variance "selisih kas" at close, kitchen tickets/queue numbers as tier features, WhatsApp receipts, QRIS MDR disclosure), ESB (esb.co.id — print types: kitchen order / table checker / bill / receipt), BEEPOS (bee.id — per-shift cashier deposit slips marketed as fraud control), Olsera, iREAP, Kasir Pintar. **Adopted:** variants+toppings as table stakes; shift cash accountability; stock change attribution; daily report + insights (avg transaction, busiest hours); honest offline tolerance; kitchen ticket as first-class object.
- **Global POS:** Square (open tickets with 500-item/180-day limits, checklist-gated Close-of-Day with owner-passcode override, offline payments auto-upload), Toast (Orders Hub lifecycle; KDS item-level fulfillment — green checks per item, ticket ready only when ALL items fulfilled; void/refund/comp as distinct audited actions; permission-gated voids with configured reason codes; skipped voided check numbers; rolling average fulfillment timer), Lightspeed KDS (New gray → Preparing blue → Ready green → Completed + On-hold brown / Canceled red; double-tap bump; 5-second undo; modified orders blink until acknowledged; FIFO oldest-left; view-only station mode; tickets show modifier CHOICE, not group name; collection code + order type on tickets), Clover (timing alerts, order-type filters, color-coded statuses), Loyverse (X-report = mid-shift snapshot that closes nothing; Z-report = close with expected cash vs counted cash vs variance, auto-prints; granular employee rights incl. "see expected cash"; KDS with wait-time color escalation, item-or-order completion, recall). **Adopted:** all of the above mechanics, mapped to single-outlet scale.
- **Open source:** OrderRestro (GitHub — "RBAC everywhere": every action a toggleable grant, never a hardcoded role check; "server-authoritative money"; once-only KOT printing; recipe deduction that never blocks a sale on shortfall; nightly cash close with variance), FloCafe (GitHub, MIT — cafe modifier model: milk type / extra shot / syrup / prep instructions; KDS waiting→preparing→ready; end-of-shift drawer reconciliation; 58/80mm thermal; "audit-ready CSV/Excel with reconciliation identities"), POSR (theoretical vs actual stock reconciliation; import = parse → validate/cleanse → human confirm → write; closing cycles with float/drop/over-short), Kasirku (Indonesian — checkout with name + table only, no account; persistent cart; order tracking), OSPOS ("cash up" EOD counting; permission groups), Odoo POS (session close posts to inventory; self-order shares menu data), Floreant (modifiers + cooking instructions + split tickets + kitchen display, stable since 2008), Chromis/uniCenta (KDS-as-remote-screen lineage), xp-thermal-service (ESC/POS from web apps). **Adopted:** grants-not-roles RBAC, server-side totals, once-only KOT, non-blocking recipe deduction, parse-validate-confirm import, no-login checkout.
- **Domain standards:** Static QRIS (verified via Bank Indonesia ecosystem sources + qris.interactive.co.id + invominder.com): one QR works from any licensed e-wallet/bank app; **customer types the nominal**; merchant confirms manually; Bank Indonesia explicitly warns merchants about fake screenshot proofs — verify own mutation, not the customer's screen. X/Z-report standard line items (mobiletransaction.org): time/date, staff ID, till ID, gross/net sales, transaction count, per-tender totals, voids, discounts, refunds, money in/out, opening float, counted cash, variance; for non-integrated payments the merchant-side total must be reconciled manually — bake this in. Recipe inventory (Loyverse advanced-inventory): auto-deduct on sale, reason-coded adjustments (damage/loss/correction), counts reconciling expected vs actual. PBJT/PB1 restaurant tax max 10%, perda-dependent, small operators commonly informal (enforcea.com) → **configurable tax rate, default 0%**.
- **UX pain points engineered against** (Reddit/Trustpilot/vendor support threads, verified): shift open/close bricking terminals (Loyverse Trustpilot) → guided wizards + never-locking local state; manual cash math at close → system-computed expected cash, cashier only counts; float entered in wrong field → read-only pre-filled expected vs input counted with sanity check; forgotten EOD → auto-close from ledger, flagged; QRIS "saldo terpotong tapi belum masuk" disputes (Dealpos support) → tri-state payment status + verification rail + proof attachment; printer deaths mid-rush → receipts as durable re-printable data + test print + WhatsApp fallback; wrong-station/duplicate prints → single station label + idempotent print log; inventory drift (r/restaurant) → theoretical vs actual side-by-side + daily quick count; invisible waste (5–10% of sales, r/restaurants) → one-tap waste logging with reason chips; role walls blocking 10-second sold-out fixes (Square) → cashier/barista availability toggle, logged; new-hire register fear (r/subway) → guided open/close checklists + practice mode.

### 1.5 Global UI/UX System Rules
- **Navigation model:** two shells. Public storefront (top bar + bottom sheets, mobile-first). Staff shell: collapsible left sidebar (icons+labels; collapses to icon rail; becomes bottom tab bar < `sm`), top bar with page title, live open/closed chip, WS connection dot, sound toggle, `Cmd/Ctrl+K` palette, user menu (role badge, logout). Role-based nav filtering; unauthorized routes → 403 view, never hidden links to pages a role can reach.
- **Palette (design tokens):** espresso `#241610` / `#3F2A1D` / `#553724` (primary buttons, headers — white/cream text); caramel gold `#C68B33` / `#D9A441` / `#E7C078` (accents, active states, highlights, ready states); cream surfaces `#FBF7F0` / `#F5EDE1`; line `#E8DECF`; ink `#201712`; muted `#857467`; success `#2F7D4F`; warning `#B26A00`; danger `#B03A2E`. Base page background white; cream only inside wrapper elements. Full dark-mode token swap via next-themes; **KDS and Board default to dark** (`#171210` bg, caramel accents). No indigo/blue anywhere.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display/headings, menu hero), Plus Jakarta Sans (UI body 14–16px), monospace for order codes, timers, amounts columns. Type scale: page 24px/600, card title 16px/600, body 14px, caption 12px.
- **Layout grid:** storefront 4-col mobile → 12-col desktop (max-w-6xl); POS/KDS/Board full-bleed; staff content pages max-w-7xl, cards `p-4`/`p-6`, `gap-4`/`gap-6`, `rounded-xl`, one elevation level. Touch targets ≥ 44px everywhere (tablet-first staff surfaces).
- **Shortcuts:** `Cmd/Ctrl+K` staff command palette (navigate, new order, X-report, reprint last receipt, search order by code, toggle availability, toggle sound); `/` focuses search; `Esc` closes any sheet/modal/drawer; arrow keys move across KDS cards, `Enter` opens; `Cmd/Ctrl+Enter` = Charge in POS; long-press = alternate touch action (KDS status menu, qty decrement).
- **Progressive disclosure:** ≤ 3 taps from any staff home screen to any core action; primary actions visible, secondary in `⋯` overflow menus, destructive always behind an AlertDialog with typed reason; power operations only via `Cmd/Ctrl+K`; POS hides all settings/admin surfaces entirely.
- **State-handling standard (every view, no exceptions):** Loading = shimmer skeletons (route-level for tables, ≥200ms delay to avoid flash); Empty = plain-language copy + single CTA ("Belum ada pesanan — buka Kasir"); Error = inline Alert + Retry + "Salin detail error"; realtime loss = persistent amber "Koneksi terputus — menyambung ulang…" banner + exponential-backoff reconnect + full refetch on recovery; optimistic updates (KDS check-offs, availability toggles) with rollback + toast on failure; autosave (POS cart per user, storefront cart in localStorage, product form drafts debounced) with dirty-state leave guard.
- **Sound conventions (all toggleable, defaults on for kitchen):** new-order chime on KDS, ready chime on Board, low-stock ping on staff shells; every sound paired with a visual (flash/blink) — never sound-only signals.
- **Printing conventions:** receipts render as print-optimized HTML views (`@media print`, 58mm/80mm CSS profiles per setting) via browser print dialog; every print action logged (order, template, actor, timestamp); reprint is always available and never duplicates ledger data.
- **Formatting:** currency `Rp 12.000` (id-ID, no decimals); timestamps `Sen, 10 Feb 14.32`; durations `12 mnt`; order codes `SKC-012`.

### 1.6 Core Data Entities (Prisma/SQLite; soft-delete where referenced)
- **User:** name, username, passwordHash, pin (nullable 4–6 digits), role (`ADMIN|CASHIER|BARISTA`), grants (JSON array overriding role bundle), isActive, isTraining, lastLoginAt.
- **Category:** name, sortOrder, isActive.
- **Product:** categoryId, name, description, imageUrl, basePrice (IDR int), unitCost (nullable), isActive (published), isAvailable (sold-out toggle), sortOrder; soft-delete.
- **VariantGroup:** productId, name ("Ukuran", "Gula", "Es", "Topping"), selectionType (`SINGLE|MULTI`), isRequired, minSelect, maxSelect, sortOrder.
- **VariantOption:** groupId, label, priceDelta (IDR int, can be 0/negative), isDefault, sortOrder, isActive.
- **Order:** code (daily `SKC-###`), type (`DINE_IN|TAKE_AWAY|DELIVERY`), status (`PENDING_PAYMENT|PENDING_VERIFICATION|PAID|PREPARING|READY|OUT_FOR_DELIVERY|COMPLETED|CANCELLED`), subtotal, deliveryFee, discountTotal, taxTotal, total, customerName, customerPhone, tableLabel, deliveryLocation, courierName, note, businessDate, cashierId, preparedById, servedById, isTraining, status timestamps (paidAt, readyAt, completedAt, cancelledAt, cancelReason).
- **OrderItem:** orderId, productId, nameSnapshot, qty, unitPrice, variantSnapshot (JSON of chosen options+deltas), lineTotal, note, checkedCount (int 0..qty), isVoided, voidReason.
- **Payment:** orderId, method (`CASH|QRIS|SPLIT`), amount, cashReceived, changeDue, qrReference (buyer name/last-4), status (`PENDING_VERIFICATION|CONFIRMED`), verifiedById, paidAt.
- **HeldOrder:** label, itemsSnapshot (JSON), createdBy, createdAt; auto-expires at business-day close; cap 20 active.
- **StockItem:** sku (unique, import key), name, unit (`gr|ml|pcs|cup|...`), qty (decimal), lowThreshold, unitCost, isActive.
- **RecipeItem:** productId, stockItemId, qtyPerUnit (in stock unit).
- **StockMovement:** stockItemId, type (`RESTOCK_IN|SALE_DEDUCT|WASTE|ADJUSTMENT|COUNT_VARIANCE|MANUAL_OUT`), qtyDelta, resultingQty, refOrderId, reason, note, userId, createdAt. Append-only.
- **StockCount:** businessDate, status (`DRAFT|APPROVED`), lines (item, systemQty, countedQty, variance), approvedById.
- **DailyReport:** businessDate, kind (`X|Z`), status (`OPEN|CLOSED`), snapshot JSON (gross, discount, void, net, cashTotal, qrisTotal, deliveryFees, taxTotal, orderCount, itemCount, byType, byHour, openingFloat, cashIn, cashOut, countedCash, expectedCash, variance), generatedById, closedAt, isAutoClosed. Z is immutable after close.
- **CashMovement:** businessDate, type (`OPENING_FLOAT|CASH_IN|CASH_OUT`), amount, reason, userId, createdAt. Append-only; feeds expected-cash math (opening float + cash payments + cash in − cash out).
- **AuditLog:** userId, action, entity, entityId, before/after JSON, createdAt. Append-only.
- **Setting:** key → JSON value (cafe profile, hours, ordering, payments, receipts, stock, tax, system).
- **DailySequence:** businessDate → lastNumber (skips voided/cancelled codes; never reused).

### 1.7 RBAC Model — Grants, Not Hardcoded Roles
- Permission engine: named grants (e.g., `order.create`, `order.voidItem`, `order.cancel`, `order.discount`, `payment.confirm`, `payment.refund`, `availability.toggle`, `product.manage`, `stock.manage`, `stock.delete`, `recipe.manage`, `count.approve`, `report.viewAll`, `data.import`, `data.report`, `user.manage`, `settings.manage`, `activity.view`). Roles are default grant bundles; per-user overrides allowed (verified OrderRestro pattern: "every action gated by an individually toggleable permission").
- Sensitive-action rules (verified Toast/Loyverse patterns): post-payment void/cancel/refund require reason + `order.cancel`/`payment.refund` grant (Admin default; Cashier/Barista off); QRIS confirmation requires `payment.confirm`; Z-report close requires `data.report` (Cashier+Admin); imports require `data.import` (Admin only); "expected cash" visibility follows `report.viewAll`.

### 1.8 Cross-Cutting System Requirements
- **Server-authoritative money:** client carts are drafts; subtotal, deltas, delivery fee, discount, tax, total recomputed server-side at order creation and payment. IDR integers only.
- **Business-day logic:** businessDate = calendar date (Asia/Jakarta) of the 07:00 opening; daily order-code sequence resets per businessDate; Z-report closes a businessDate; past unclosed dates auto-close from ledger on next access, flagged `isAutoClosed` (verified pain fix for forgotten EOD).
- **QRIS manual-verification protocol (no gateway):** Admin uploads static merchant QR image + merchant name in Settings. Customer-side flow displays the exact nominal BIG (static QR = customer types the amount — verified), then "Saya sudah bayar" → `PENDING_VERIFICATION` in the cashier verification rail; cashier confirms only after checking the merchant app mutation (inline reminder: "Periksa mutasi di aplikasi merchant — bukti tangkapan layar bisa dipalsukan" per Bank Indonesia warning); optional buyer reference captured; verified totals reconcile against a merchant-app total input field inside the Z-report wizard. Split = cash portion + QRIS remainder, both recorded as Payment rows.
- **Audit everything:** every mutation (orders, items, payments, stock, products, settings, users, imports, Z-closes) writes AuditLog with actor + before/after.
- **Realtime:** socket.io mini-service (dedicated port, gateway via `?XTransformPort=`); events: `order:created`, `order:status`, `order:itemChecked`, `payment:updated`, `stock:low`, `stock:movement`, `menu:availability`, `day:closed`. KDS flash+chime+blink-until-acknowledge on new/modified orders; Board and Track pages live-update.
- **Inventory integrity:** recipe deduction fires at order `READY` (configurable to `PAID`), computed from non-voided lines only; **never blocks a sale on shortfall** — shows a warning chip instead (verified OrderRestro guard); every movement attributed and append-only; theoretical vs actual reconciled via counts.
- **Practice mode:** Admin-enabled sandbox sessions (banner "MODE LATIHAN", amber); training orders watermarked `isTraining`, excluded from reports, stock, and Z; one-tap reset.
- **Auth & security:** session cookie; argon2/bcrypt hashes; login rate limit (5 fails → 30s cooldown); PIN pad quick-login for shared tablets; no email flows; zod validation on every API route; no PII beyond optional name/phone.
- **Backups:** full Excel/JSON export from /data; SQLite file in `/db`; pre-import automatic snapshot.
- **Deployment note:** route map below is logical; if the hosting target exposes a single entry route, render the same map as client-side views under `/` with URL-query state — behavior identical.
